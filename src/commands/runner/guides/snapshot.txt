# Reading the page by ref

snapshot prints the page's url and title, then its accessibility outline, one element per line, such as `- button "Save" [ref=e4]`. It is plain text on stdout, so read it as it stands or pipe it. --interactive keeps only what a user can act on, which is shorter and usually enough to pick a ref.

A ref belongs to the latest snapshot. After a navigation, after running code, or when ref answers ref-not-found, snapshot again.

no-page and runner-cannot-snapshot-pages exit 2: open a page first, or launch a playwright runner. page-snapshots-not-enabled exits 2 and means your team does not have this yet; use screenshot and act instead. no-answer and runner-unreachable exit 4 and are worth retrying.
